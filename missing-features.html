How to add a missing landscape film poster

Find the film in the list below. Give its landscape poster exactly the suggested filename, then upload it to the OneDrive folder landscape_extra_films.

After synchronization, the system matches the filename to the DX film title and places the poster chronologically by screening time in both Films Landscape and Landscape All.

Use JPG/JPEG or PNG. The suggested filename uses .jpg; if the image is PNG, use .png instead. Keep only one manual poster per film title. Unmatched posters remain alphabetically ordered after the DX films. If DX later provides a landscape poster, it takes priority.
